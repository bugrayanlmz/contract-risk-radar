Fictional landlord Reese rents a home to fictional tenant Jules. Monthly rent is USD 1300.00. The lease begins December 1, 2027 and ends November 30, 2028.

When rent arrives late, a one-time fee of 5 percent of monthly rent applies after 2 calendar days of grace; that fee does not accrue daily.

If rent remains unpaid for more than 20 calendar days, an additional late fee of USD 10.00 accrues daily until payment.

The tenant must pay for structural repairs, even when the tenant did not cause the damage.

The tenant bears losses arising from landlord negligence; no overall liability cap is stated.
