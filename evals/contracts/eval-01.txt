The fictional tenant Ellis rents a dwelling from fictional landlord Sky. The lease starts on February 1, 2027 and ends on January 31, 2028. Monthly rent is USD 1200.00 and the security deposit is USD 1200.00.

Renewal is not automatic; a signed written extension is required.

Early termination is allowed with a fee of half a month's rent; unused future rent is not payable.

The deposit is refundable within 10 calendar days after move-out, with deductions limited to documented damage.

A one-time fee of 2 percent of monthly rent applies after 7 calendar days of grace and does not accrue daily.

No rent increase is permitted during the stated term.

The landlord may terminate for cause only, with 45 calendar days of notice.

Tenant liability is capped at damage caused by the tenant and excludes landlord negligence, structural repairs, and normal wear.
